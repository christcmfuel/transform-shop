Recommendation

If the goal is more free-session bookings, pick Together. It is the shortest route from arrival to booking of the five (hero button, then the whole week on screen two, then the plans on screen three, with a sticky bar whose price button stays on the page), and four of the five judges gave it their top mark independently. Its one middling score, 7 on distinctiveness, is fair: it is Neighbours' card vocabulary with the flourishes removed, and its headline is a borrowed line where the gym already has its own. If the goal is standing out, Sessions is the honest answer rather than Damn. Damn is the only page nobody in Plymouth looks like, but it pays for that with a 5 on the booking path and a 5 on mobile, and the voice judge's fix (drop the serif) would remove the very thing that earned its 9, so choosing Damn means accepting the boutique costume as it is. Sessions scored 7 or 8 on every lens, the timetable-as-hero is a real idea that reads as the gym's own, and its three fixes are small. Has Together earned its place over an original? On the evidence, yes: its 1.0 lead over Sessions is larger than any round-to-round swing we have seen (0.2 to 0.4), so it is not panel noise. But two things should be said plainly before you commit. First, it is the least complete page: no HYROX block, no personal training block, no FAQ, no gallery. That is why it is nine phone screens instead of fifteen, and it means the subpages have to carry the rest; if Plymouth's official HYROX club is a reason people join, Together currently gives it one sentence. Second, its brand-voice 9 was earned by the sentences, not the headline; the gym's own line should come back. Our recommendation: choose Together for the homepage, apply its three fixes and add a short HYROX block below the plans, and keep Sessions as the fallback if you want the page to be more memorable than it is warm.

Pairing with the kit store

Together and Neighbours both pair with People (warm rounded): they share the paper palette, Outfit display type, green pill buttons and Cosmo as a recurring character, so the store would read as the same shop. Sessions pairs with Field (olive utility): ink borders, mono labels, 6px radii and a timetable-like grid are the same language. Damn pairs with Chalk (light serif): Instrument Serif over IBM Plex Mono on paper with hairline rules is Chalk's register exactly. Floor pairs with Blackout (dark video): black, neon green and condensed caps. If Together is chosen, People is the store; if Sessions, Field.

Since the first panel

Across the three rounds: Sessions went 6.8, 8.0, 7.6; Neighbours 7.0, 7.2, 7.4; Damn 6.8, 7.2, 6.4; Floor 5.4, 4.8, 4.6; Together is new at 8.6. The panel's lenses were not identical each round, so a shift of 0.2 to 0.4 is within noise, and Neighbours' steady small gains (the self-hiding sticky bar, the menu engineering, plans on screen five) and Sessions' slip (a fix pass that polished the middle with compact plan cards and a collapsed comparison but left the timetable foot as text links and the hero's off-page button) are both of that size. Damn's 0.8 drop is not noise: two judges independently hit the same things the earlier rounds had already flagged and the fixes did not touch, the sticky price button leaving the site, the plans on screen ten, and a 21-screen phone page. Floor's hero now renders, so the round-two artefact explanation is gone, and it still finished last by a distance; its problems are order and register, not capture. The pattern across the fix passes is that they addressed craft (tap targets, clamp floors, reduced motion, sticky bars) and largely left structure alone (where the plans sit, where the sticky price goes, how long the phone page is), and structure is what four of the five lenses score. Together earned its 8.6 by being built from what the earlier rounds taught rather than by being patched, which is the strongest argument that it has earned its place, and also the reason its fixes are the easiest to apply.

What only you can supply

To lift every option

  • Coach headshots and roles. Ask for one square photo per coach (Narcisse, Eliya, Kirsty, Brad, Ava; same background, 800px+) plus a five-word role line each (e.g. 'Kirsty · Pilates and Foundations', 'Brad · HYROX and PT'). Every option currently shows names only, with 'Coach' under each and initial-letter avatars on Neighbours and Together. With faces and roles: the coach strips become real cards, the Foundations CTA can say who runs it, and the PT section can name the coach you would actually get.
  • Member photo consent and four new shots. The group-under-the-wall Instagram post (18138186763548491) is the hero on Neighbours, Sessions and Together and shows about 25 identifiable faces; the HYROX finishers, the squat and the circuits shots are also reused everywhere. Ask Chris to confirm each person agreed to website use (or a signed release), and to shoot four landscape frames at 1600px+: the entrance and car park from Meadow Close, the front desk, a Foundations class mid-session, a Tuesday Strength morning. No option shows the way in or a beginner being coached.
  • One line per class, with duration and who it suits, for Circuits, Strength, HIIT, Yoga, Pilates, Foundations and HYROX, plus who coaches each. Only Foundations is described on any page (Damn adds a sentence for Circuits and Yoga). With this, timetable slots become tappable with a description, the filter chips on Neighbours/Sessions/Together get a caption, and the 'Do I need to be fit?' FAQ can answer per class.
  • The full weekly timetable from PushPress, including the HIIT start times (Mon, Wed, Fri) and every HYROX session. All five grids show 15 sessions against a '20+ classes a week' stat, tell people to phone for HIIT, and have no HYROX slot despite selling 'official HYROX training club' in a headline section. One export removes every 'ring 01752 427413 for the time' and makes the number on the page match the grid.
  • Staffed hours (days and times the desk is manned) and what changes outside them. Every option says the £10 day pass is 'staffed hours only' and Neighbours' FAQ says 'for staffed hours, ring', but no page states them. Needed for the day-pass line, the free session, 'say hello at the front desk', kit collection, and openingHoursSpecification in the JSON-LD.
  • What the free session actually is. The Calendly is 'mike-transformgyms/free-trial-session', so is it a coached class of their choice, a floor induction, or 30 minutes with Mike? How long, what to bring, who meets them, minimum age, and whether the desk can book it too. Sessions says 'Pick a session, turn up', Together says 'Book a slot', Damn says 'Any day'; none says what happens. This fills the 'what to expect' steps on Neighbours/Together, the trial bands, and Damn's Day 0.
  • Plan small print and the members app. Yearly plan prices, any joining fee, notice period behind 'cancel anytime', whether Hybrid's 12 sessions roll over, direct-debit day, concessions (student, NHS, forces, couples), under-18 policy, and the app's real name with store links (the Hub runs on PushPress, so presumably its member app) and what it does (book classes, door entry, payments). Each option lists 'Members app' as a feature with no name and no 'how you book a class' line.
  • Founder story and HYROX dates. Two paragraphs on who Mike and his partner are, why 'the gym that actually gives a damn', and the Nov 2025 opening, plus the next HYROX event the club is training for and any in-house race day. Only the baseline has the 'we built Transform because Plymouth deserved better' line; none of the five has an owner's voice, and the HYROX sections are generic (8 stations, 8km) with nothing dated to train towards.

Still weak on all four

Known gaps

  • The headline stat and the timetable disagree on every page: '20+ classes a week' sits beside a grid of 15 sessions, hedged with 'core classes shown'. HYROX is a headline section on four of five (a footer line on Together) yet never appears in the week, so a HYROX-curious visitor cannot see when to come.
  • HIIT runs three evenings a week and no option gives a time; each one sends a first-timer to the phone (or to a schedule page that also just says 'Evening'). The most likely after-work class for a beginner is the one with no time on it.
  • 'Staffed hours' is referenced by all five for the £10 day pass but stated nowhere; a non-member has no opening hours at all, and none of the five JSON-LD blocks carries openingHoursSpecification.
  • The free session is undefined and inconsistent: booked through one person's Calendly in a new tab, and the five pages describe it as 'pick a session, turn up', 'book a slot', 'any day' or 'walk in'. No page says what you will do, how long it lasts or who you will meet, which is the single question a nervous beginner has.
  • Reviews: the 'Read all reviews' link on every page is g.page/r/…/review, which is Google's write-a-review URL, not the listing; '5 stars on Google' has no review count; one quote is attributed to 'Google Review (verified)'; and three quotes say 'Member since 2024' while the Hub's own analytics date the opening to Nov 2025. Verify or drop the years.
  • Coaches are names only: no faces, no roles, 'Coach' five times, and on Neighbours and Together a coloured initial that reads as a placeholder. For pages built on 'everyone knows your name', nobody who works there is shown.
  • Wayfinding and hero imagery: three options share the same portrait Instagram group shot (on phones the people sit behind the text), Floor opens on an empty gym, Damn on a single lifter. None shows a beginner in a coached class, the entrance or a map; for a unit on Langage Industrial Estate, '2 minutes off the A38' and a Maps link are the only directions.
  • Launch leftovers in all five files: <meta name="robots" content="noindex">, '· Option A–E' in the <title>, the fixed 'Options' pill, and every nav and footer link pointing at the old-design inner pages (membership.html, schedule.html, personal-training.html). Whichever wins will hand visitors to pages that look nothing like it.
