Recommendation

If the goal is more free-session bookings, pick Together. It scored 9 on first-visit clarity, path to the free session, brand voice and mobile, it is the shortest page (10 phone screens against 21 for Damn and Floor), and a phone visitor sees the headline, price, address and one green button without scrolling. Its only 7 is distinctiveness, and that is fixable without changing its shape (real coach photos, less card chrome). If the goal is standing out, pick Damn: the craft judge gave it the only 9 on the panel and no Plymouth gym looks like it. But be clear about the trade: it is 21 phone screens with the timetable nine screens down, the mobile judge gave it a 6, and the voice judge thinks the serif register dresses The People's Gym as a boutique studio, so you would be choosing memorability over the beginner on a phone. Sessions is the honest middle (8 on clarity, path, mobile and craft) if you want the timetable to be the brand. On stability: Together has led by exactly 1.0 for two consecutive rounds and no judge scored it below 7, so the top pick is safe to decide on now. The order underneath is less settled (Damn has just overtaken Neighbours after its fixes, and Sessions and Damn are 0.2 apart), so do not treat second place as decided. Where judges disagreed: Damn split widest (6 on mobile, 9 on craft, and the voice judge's fix, drop the serif, would undo the craft judge's score); Neighbours split 8 on voice against 6 on clarity and craft; Floor sat at 5 to 7 everywhere, meaning on voice and craft it is no better than what is live.

Pairing with the kit store

Together and Neighbours pair with People (warm-rounded): same paper background, rounded cards, Outfit headings and Cosmo thread, so the store feels like a room of the same gym. Sessions pairs with Field (olive-utility): its mono labels, colour-coded slots and listings-page logic sit naturally beside an olive utility catalogue. Damn pairs with Chalk (light-serif): the Instrument Serif italics, hairlines and mono captions are the same editorial system, and the kit strip already reads like a Chalk product page. Floor pairs with Blackout (dark-video): black ground, neon green, condensed caps and the giant 'Transform' footer are the Blackout look. If you choose Together for the site, the store choice is People; if you choose Damn, it is Chalk.

Since the first panel

Against round 3 (Together 8.6, Sessions 7.6, Neighbours 7.4, Damn 6.4, Floor 4.6) the leaders held and the tail moved. Together stayed at 8.6: the HYROX block and hero facts row were noticed (the clarity judge quotes the facts row as the reason for the 9) but they did not lift the craft score, which is still the 7 that caps it. Sessions stayed at 7.6 with the same shape of scores; its fixes were tidy-ups, not changes to the first screen, and the first screen is still what holds it back. Damn rose a full point to 7.4, and the fixes earned it: moving the plans to 3.5 screens and anchoring the sticky price on-page lifted clarity and the path to 8 and 7, while the craft judge's 9 was already there. Floor rose 1.2 to 5.8, and that too was earned by the cut timeline and the sticky bar finally carrying 'Free session', but it crosses the baseline of 5 only on average; on voice and craft it is still no better than what is live, and the Options pill left on the phone hero cost it on two lenses. Neighbours fell 0.6 to 6.8 and is the one option whose fixes did not pay: its plans still arrive after the three 'walk in' steps at 4.5 screens, its phone hero still hides the address, and with the others tightened up the judges marked its chip chrome and template devices harder. Across four rounds the picture has gone from a close three-way tie (round 1: 7.0, 6.8, 6.8) to a clear leader with a 1.0 gap, and that gap has now survived two rounds of fixes, which is the strongest sign the ranking is real rather than an artefact of one pass.

What only you can supply

To lift every option

  • Photos with consent. Ask for: a headshot of each of Narcisse, Eliya, Kirsty, Brad and Ava (square, 800px+), their role in one line, and which classes each one coaches; plus 5 to 8 candid member shots with a signed consent line per identifiable face, and confirmation that the group-under-the-wall shot (already the hero on neighbours, sessions and together) is cleared. Every page would swap the initials-only coach cards for faces, add 'with Kirsty' under timetable slots, and put real people behind the five testimonials.
  • One paragraph per class for Circuits, Strength, HIIT, Yoga, Pilates and Foundations: what actually happens, how long it runs, the level, who coaches it, whether you need to book, class cap. Only Foundations is described on any page ('beginner-friendly intro class'). Every timetable would gain a line under each slot, the filter note could say what the class is, and damn's first-week itinerary would stop guessing ('the coach keeps the room moving').
  • HIIT times for Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and a check that the rest of the grid is current. All five timetables print 'Evening · ring 01752 427413' three times; it is the only slot with no time and the one an evening-only beginner is most likely to want. One fact removes three phone links per page.
  • HYROX specifics: the weekly HYROX session day and time (it is not on any timetable), the next race the club is training for and its date, how many members have raced, and whether a complete beginner can join. Every page calls it 'Plymouth's official HYROX training club' then says 'ask the team' and points the 'Train with us' button at the generic free-session Calendly. A date and a slot would make the claim concrete.
  • Staffed hours, how 24/7 access works (fob, app, induction on first visit?), and the name of the members app with its App Store and Play links. 'Members app' appears in every plan list on every page and is never named; 'day pass, staffed hours only, ask at reception' appears everywhere with no hours. This would fill the day-pass note, the FAQ on damn and floor, an openingHours line in the JSON-LD, and turn the app bullet into a link.
  • What the free session actually is: a coached class from the timetable, a floor tour plus a workout, or a one-to-one? How long, who meets you at the door, what to bring, whether showers and lockers exist, and whether Foundations counts as the free session. Every page's hint says only 'It's free, and it takes a minute. Opens our booking page.' The hero hint, the three-step 'first visit' blocks (neighbours, together), the Day 0 row (damn) and the FAQs would all be written from this.
  • Plan fine print and PT price: notice period behind 'cancel anytime', any joining fee, the yearly plan prices ('Yearly plans available' is on every page with no number), whether unused Hybrid sessions roll over, whether you can freeze, and the personal training rate. The plan cards, the plan-note line, sessions' comparison table and neighbours' 'which plan?' helper all stop one sentence short without these.
  • Founder and proof facts: Mike's name and a three-sentence story (why the gym opened, when), the Google review count behind the 5-star claim, and confirmation of the testimonial attributions. Four quotes say 'Member since 2024' on every page, but the Hub's own notes record the gym opening in Nov 2025; if that is right the dates are wrong on all five options. A founder line would give the 'gives a damn' headline a person behind it; a review count would make '5 stars' credible.

Still weak on all four

Known gaps

  • HIIT has no time on any page. Each timetable prints 'Evening · ring 01752 427413' three times, and the class filters on neighbours, sessions and together will happily report 'HIIT: 3 sessions a week' for a class whose time the page does not know.
  • HYROX is the headline credential but never appears on the timetable, has no date, and its only route is 'ask the team'. Every 'Train with us' button opens the same generic free-session Calendly, so a HYROX-curious visitor gets nothing more than a beginner does.
  • Coaches are five first names and the word 'Coach'. No faces, no roles, no link between a coach and a slot. damn even says 'You'll meet the coaches on your first visit', which is the page admitting it cannot introduce them.
  • The free session is never explained. Every CTA opens Calendly in a new tab with 'It's free, and it takes a minute'. A beginner does not learn whether they will be in a class, on the floor, or with one coach, nor what to wear or bring.
  • '20+ classes a week' sits beside a grid that shows 15 slots, and every page hedges with 'Core classes shown. Full list on the schedule page.' The number and the evidence disagree on the same screen.
  • The members app is unnamed in every plan list, and 'day pass, staffed hours only, ask at reception' appears everywhere with no staffed hours, so the two things a non-member can actually use (a £10 day pass, a free PT consultation at reception) have no known window.
  • Proof is the same five quotes with initials avatars on every page. Four are dated 'Member since 2024', which predates the Nov 2025 opening recorded in the Hub's notes. There is no review count, and pages use two different review URLs (a Maps search for 'Read all the reviews', g.page for 'Leave a review').
  • Nothing on first-visit practicalities beyond parking: no photo of the entrance or building, no embedded map, no showers/lockers/changing rooms, no PT price, no notice period behind 'cancel anytime', and no yearly price behind 'Yearly plans available'.
