Straight answers

Before you walk in.

The things people ask before their first visit. Anything else, call 01752 427413.

Do I need to book my first session?
Yes, and it's free. Pick a slot, walk in, train hard. No commitment, no hard sell.
I've never trained before. Where do I start?
Foundations, Wednesday at 6:30pm. It's our beginner-friendly intro class and the best first step.
Is the gym really open 24/7?
Yes. Every plan includes 24/7 gym floor access, from Gym Only at £39.99 a month upwards.
Can I cancel?
Anytime. No hidden fees on any plan. Yearly plans are also available if you'd rather commit.
Can I just drop in for a day?
A day pass is £10 and gets you on the floor.
Which plan includes classes?
Hybrid (£65) gives you 12 group sessions a month with all class types included. Unlimited (£90) is unlimited classes with HYROX, yoga and Pilates included. Compare plans.
Where do I park?
On site, free. We're at 9B Meadow Close on Langage Industrial Estate, 2 minutes off the A38.
Do you train for HYROX?
We're Plymouth's official HYROX training club. Eight stations, 8 km of running, one finish line, and coaches who'll get you race-ready.
Is there a members app?
Yes, it comes with every plan. Coffee and supplements are on site too.
Where do I get kit?
The official kit store. Everything is printed and embroidered to order and collected from the front desk.
