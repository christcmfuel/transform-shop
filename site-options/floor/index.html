Straight answers

Before you walk in.

The things people ask before their first visit. Anything else, call 01752 427413.

How do I get my free first session?
Your first session is on us. Grab a slot on the link, then walk in, train hard, feel the difference. No commitment, no hard sell.
I've never trained before. Where do I start?
Foundations, Wednesday at 6:30pm. It's our beginner-friendly intro class and the best first step.
Do I really get 24/7 gym access?
Yes. Every membership includes 24/7 gym floor access, from Gym Only at £39.99 a month upwards. The £10 day pass is the exception: it covers staffed hours only.
Can I cancel?
Anytime. No hidden fees on any plan. Yearly plans are also available if you'd rather commit.
Can I just drop in for a day?
Yes. A day pass is £10, pay per visit, and covers the gym during staffed hours rather than 24/7. Ask at reception or call ahead.
Which plan includes classes?
Hybrid (£65): 12 group sessions a month, any class type (HYROX, yoga and Pilates included). Unlimited (£90): the same classes, unlimited. Compare plans.
Where do I park?
On site, free. We're at 9B Meadow Close on Langage Industrial Estate, 2 minutes off the A38.
Do you train for HYROX?
We're Plymouth's official HYROX training club. Eight stations, 8 km of running, one finish line, and coaches who'll get you race-ready.
Is there a members app?
Yes, it comes with every plan. Coffee and supplements are on site too.
Where do I get kit?
The official kit store. Everything is printed and embroidered to order and collected from the front desk.
Do you do personal training?
Yes. Customised one-to-one sessions with nutrition advice, progress tracking and ongoing support, for all levels. It starts with a free consultation: speak to a member of the team or read more.
