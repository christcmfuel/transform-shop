01 · Why Transform

More than a gym. A community.

The People's Gym. Coaches who know their stuff, members who cheer each other on, and a floor that is open whenever you need it.

Transform is a place where every person, at every level, feels genuinely welcome from day one. Beginners, HYROX athletes, and everyone in between train side by side here, and nobody is watching the mirror.

09 · Questions

Before you ask.

The short answers. Anything else, call 01752 427413 or ask at the front desk.

Is there parking?
Yes, free parking on site. We're on Langage Industrial Estate, 2 minutes off the A38.
Is it really open 24/7?
The gym floor is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
Can I just try it?
Two ways. Your first session is free, and after that a day pass is £10.
What if I want to cancel?
Cancel anytime. No hidden fees on any plan. Compare memberships.
I'm a complete beginner. Where do I start?
Foundations, Wednesday at 6:30pm. It's the beginner-friendly intro class and the best first step onto the floor.
Do you train for HYROX?
We're Plymouth's official HYROX training club. HYROX is included in the Unlimited plan and covered by Hybrid's group sessions.
