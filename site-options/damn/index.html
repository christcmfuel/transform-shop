Questions

Before you ask.

The short answers. Anything else, call 01752 427413 or ask at the front desk.

Is there parking?
Yes, free parking on site. We're on Langage Industrial Estate, 2 minutes off the A38.
Is it really 24/7?
Members get 24/7 gym floor access, 7 days a week. Day passes cover staffed hours only.
Can I just try it?
Two ways. Your first session is free. After that a day pass is £10, pay per visit, for access during staffed hours. Ask at reception or call ahead.
What if I want to cancel?
Cancel anytime. No hidden fees on any plan. Compare memberships.
I'm a complete beginner. Where do I start?
Foundations, Wednesday at 6:30pm. It's the beginner-friendly intro class and the best first step onto the floor.
Do you train for HYROX?
We're Plymouth's official HYROX training club. HYROX training is covered by both Hybrid and Unlimited.
Do you do personal training?
Yes. Customised one-to-one sessions with nutrition advice, progress tracking and ongoing support, for all levels. It starts with a free consultation. Personal training.
Which plan includes the classes?
Hybrid gives you 12 group sessions a month across all class types. Unlimited gives you every class, every week. Both include 24/7 gym floor access.
