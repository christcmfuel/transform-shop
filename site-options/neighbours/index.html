Good to know

Questions people ask before they visit.

If yours isn't here, ring us on 01752 427413 or email info@transformgyms.uk.

Do I need to be fit already?

No. Foundations is a beginner-friendly intro class every Wednesday at 6:30pm. Start here. No egos. No judgement.

What happens at the free session?

Your first session is on us. Walk in, train hard, feel the difference. No commitment, no hard sell.

Book a free session

When is the gym open?

Members have 24/7 access to the gym floor on every plan. Classes run at the times on the timetable, seven days a week.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking, always. We're at 9B Meadow Close on Langage Industrial Estate, two minutes off the A38.

Which plan includes classes?

Hybrid (£65 a month) includes 12 group sessions a month with all class types. Unlimited (£90 a month) includes unlimited group classes with HYROX, yoga and Pilates. Gym Only (£39.99 a month) is the 24/7 floor without classes.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel anytime, no hidden fees. Yearly plans are available too, and a day pass is £10 if you'd rather pay as you go.

Is there a members app?

Yes. The members app is included on every plan.

Do you train for HYROX?

We're Plymouth's official HYROX training club. HYROX is 8 workout stations, 8 km of running and 1 finish line, and it's included on the Unlimited plan.

Is there coffee?

Coffee and supplements are on site.

Can I get the kit?

Official kit is at shop.tcmfuel.com. It's printed and embroidered to order and collected from the front desk.
